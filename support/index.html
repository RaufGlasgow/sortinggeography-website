Support

Something not working, a number that looks wrong, or a question? Write to us.

Contact

Email support@sortinggeography.com.

If you are reporting a problem, these help us find it:

  • what you were doing when it happened;
  • what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • your phone or tablet model, whether it runs Android or iOS, and which version;
  • the app's version, shown in Settings.

Common questions

How do I play?

Each puzzle shows five countries and one question, such as which has the longest coastline. Drag them into order, then confirm. Your score fills in on the same board, with the real numbers and any pairs you had the wrong way round.

Is it free?

Yes. Every question, every country and the Daily are free. The Daily never shows an ad. Endless play shows an occasional ad between puzzles.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play. There is nothing to sign in to.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The countries, flags and numbers are all inside the app, so you can play without a connection.

How do I move my progress to a new phone or tablet?

Your progress is stored on your device, not on a server of ours, so it moves only with your device's own backup and restore through Google or iCloud. We cannot restore it for you, because we never have it.

Where do the numbers come from?

Every value comes from one named primary source, with its year and a link, shown in the game. The full list is on the data sources page.

I think a number is wrong

Please tell us: email the country, the question, and what you think the figure should be, with a source if you have one, and we will check it against the source.

What does the app collect?

Very little, and nothing that identifies you. The privacy page says exactly what, including what Google's ad service receives.

Does the app send usage numbers?

No. It sends no usage statistics of its own. The privacy page says what Google's ad service receives. Settings has a switch, Share anonymous usage numbers. This version of the game does not send any usage numbers yet, whatever the switch is set to.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything the game keeps is on your device, and it goes with the app.
