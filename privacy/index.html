Privacy

Sorting Geography is a game about countries. It is not interested in you.

Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, and the game never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your phone or tablet.
  • Ads appear only in Endless play, never in the Daily. Every player is treated as possibly a child, so ads are never personalised and nothing tracks you across other apps.
  • The app sends no usage statistics of its own.

No account, no sign-in

There is no account to create and no way to sign in. The game does not ask for your name, your email address or your phone number, because it has no use for any of them.

Your progress stays on your device

The puzzles you have played, the countries you have unlocked, your scores and your settings are stored on your phone or tablet, and nowhere else. If your device backs itself up through Google or iCloud, that backup is between you and Google or Apple; it does not pass through us, and there is no server of ours holding it. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

Playing needs no connection

All the country data ships inside the app, so playing needs no connection and nothing you do waits on one. The app uses the internet only to show ads.

Advertising

Endless play shows an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles. The Daily never shows an ad, and neither does the first guided puzzle. Ads are served by Google's AdMob.

Every player is treated as possibly a child. The app does not ask your age. Instead, every ad request tells Google to treat the player as a child, and to show only ads suitable for all ages. That means:

  • ads are never personalised, and nothing builds a profile of you for advertising;
  • the app does not use your device's advertising ID. On Android the permission to read it is removed from the app; on iOS the app never asks to track you;
  • nothing links what you do in this game to what you do in other apps.

To show an ad, count it and stop fraud, Google's software still receives some information automatically: your IP address (from which a rough location can be estimated), how you interact with the ad, basic performance information about the app, and a device identifier that is not the advertising ID. That goes to Google, not to us, and Google handles it under its own policy: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics

The app sends no usage statistics of its own: nothing about how you play is sent to us. Apart from a result you choose to share, the only data that leaves the device is what Google's ad software receives, described above.

Sharing a result

When you share a result, the app opens your device's own share sheet with a short text, and you choose where it goes. The app does not see who you send it to.

No crash reporting

The app contains no crash-reporting service. If something goes wrong, Google Play and the App Store may pass the developer an anonymous crash report, under the settings you chose on your device.

This website

The website has no analytics and no ads, and sets no cookies of its own. It is served through Cloudflare, which handles the connection and therefore sees your IP address, as any web host does.

The home page has a box where you can leave an email address to be told when the game ships. It is optional, and it is the only thing the site ever asks for. If you leave an address, we store the address, the date you left it, which page you left it on and that page's language, then the date our note to you was sent (or that it could not be sent), and nothing else. You get a note straight away confirming it, then a message when the game ships and when new ways to sort are added. Nothing else, and we never sell or share it. The mail is sent through Resend, which handles delivery on our behalf and therefore also sees the address. Every message carries an unsubscribe link, and using it deletes the address rather than marking it inactive. You can also ask us to delete it at any time by writing to support@sortinggeography.com.

Children

The game is suitable for children and is built for them as well as for adults: no chat, no content from other players, no social features, nothing that asks for personal information, and the ad settings described above for every player.

Changes

If what the app or the site collects changes, this page changes with it before the new version is released, and the date at the top moves.

Contact

Questions about any of this are welcome: support@sortinggeography.com. More help is on the support page.
