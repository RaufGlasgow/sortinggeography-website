Put the world in order

Sorting Geography is a geography game for phones and tablets, on Android and iOS. You drag five countries into order by one fact, such as coastline, population or highest point, and every number comes from a named source.

How to play

  1. 1

    Read the question

    Each round asks one thing, like Coastline (km). Five countries, one right order.

  2. 2

    Drag them into order

    Longest at the top, shortest at the bottom. Move them until it looks right.

  3. 3

    Confirm and learn

    Your score fills in on the same board, with the real numbers.

One Daily, the same for everyone

Once you have played a while, the Daily opens: one new puzzle a day, the same five countries and the same question for every player. Share your result without giving the answer away.

Every number has a source

Every value comes from one named primary source. The data sources page lists each one. Where a source has no figure for a country, that country is left out of the question rather than guessed.

Unlock the world as you play

You start with countries most people know and unlock the rest as you play. Five levels, Beginner to Expert.

Questions

Is Sorting Geography free?

Yes, it is free to play. The Daily never shows ads. Endless play can show ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The countries, flags and numbers are all in the app, so you can play without a connection.

Where do the numbers come from?

From one named primary source per value. The full list is on the data sources page.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play.
