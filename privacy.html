Privacy

Sorting Geography is a game about countries. It is not interested in you.

Draft — not yet final. The app has not been released. This page will be finalised against the shipping build before submission, and it must describe the app exactly, because an app-store privacy declaration that does not match the binary is a rejection.

No account, no sign-in

There is no account to create and no way to sign in. The game does not ask for your name, your email address or your phone number, because it has no use for any of them.

Your progress stays on your device

Puzzles you have played, countries you have unlocked, your streak and your scores are stored on your phone. If your device backs itself up through iCloud or Google, that backup is between you and Apple or Google — it does not pass through us, and there is no server of ours holding it.

The game works offline

All the country data ships inside the app. Playing a puzzle sends nothing anywhere and needs no connection.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which questions are too hard or where people give up, the app records anonymous events — that a puzzle was finished, on which measure, at which difficulty. These carry no name, no email, no advertising identifier and no location, and cannot be tied back to a person. They will be listed precisely here before release.

Advertising

The daily puzzle never carries an advertisement. Elsewhere the free version may show them, and a paid unlock removes them entirely. Any advertising SDK used will be named on this page, along with what it can see, before the app ships.

Children

The game is suitable for children and is built accordingly: no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and nothing that asks a child for personal information.

Contact

Questions about any of this are welcome, and the contact address will be published here before release.
