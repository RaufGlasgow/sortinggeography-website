Become a tester

Help us test Sorting Geography on Android before it goes public.

Why we need you

Before Sorting Geography goes public, we are testing it with real players. This test runs on Google Play, so it is for Android only.

What you need

  • an Android phone or tablet with Google Play;
  • a Google account, the same one your phone uses for Google Play.

How to join

  1. Join the testers' group, signed in with your Google account: testers' group: link to come.
  2. On your phone or tablet, open the test link and tap "Become a tester": Google Play test: link to come.
  3. From that page, install Sorting Geography from Google Play. Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app's.

What testers do

  • Keep the game installed and stay in the test for at least 14 days in a row.
  • Play a few games every day.
  • After you have played for a while, a Daily appears at the bottom of the screen. From then on, play it once a day too.
  • Tell us anything that seems wrong, however small.

Tell us what you think

Anything confusing, broken, too easy or too hard: email support@sortinggeography.com. A screenshot helps. So does your phone or tablet model.

Your details

When you join the testers' group, we can see the Google address you joined with. We use it only to run this test. The game itself collects no more from testers than from anyone else; the privacy page says what that is.
